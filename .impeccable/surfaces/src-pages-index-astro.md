# Home page

Mode: Read. Route: /. Built for open-source peers who arrive from a repo and want to know who is behind it; they leave for a project, the résumé, GitHub, or email. Name and work share the first viewport. The user asked for precise and quiet. Start fresh: the Archive wall and Index attempts are spent. TASA is optional, but correct Hanji and Tâi-lô/POJ rendering is required.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a type foundry's specimen sheet. The type scale is the Chinese 號 size system, and openers are labelled with their size in the margin. It refuses the hero, the card grid, and the dark terminal look.

OWN-WORLD: White sheet, near-black ink, lead grey for metadata, and non-repro proofing blue for size labels, section numbers, and links only. A heavy Hanji serif at 初號, a SIL reading serif for Latin with reliable stacked marks, and MonoLisa for labels. One vertical margin rule carries the size labels. Thin hairlines between entries; no boxes, shadows, or icons.

STORY: Visitors read the name down the size ladder (kahiok, 莊加旭, Tsng Kahiok), learn what kahiok does in three lines, and scan §-numbered work. Each populated section ends in one action. The empty Writing state is exempt until posts exist. The colophon closes with a tone-mark specimen line that proves the type.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Head: 字樣 in the margin, then kahiok.com with its set date, and § nav with section counts. Left: the margin rule carries 初號 42pt (with the enlargement, 放大 ×1.5 or ×1.25; none on phones), 一號 27.5pt, 三號 15.75pt and 五號 10.5pt labels, set against kahiok (~108px at 1440), 莊加旭 (~71px, weight 900) and Tsng Kahiok (~41px). The bio and links follow. Right: §1 Work, entries 1.1–1.7. Every section opener carries its 三號 label under its § number. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing an entry snaps its number to blue and reveals 四號 13.75pt under it, with no tween. On phones, §1 and the first entry sit above 844px. The page is light only, a white sheet.

FORM: Type specimen, 4th of the re-roll 1 grounded list, seed:b9acf2ab9a411a3c reroll 1. Raises: numbered links (patent), one action per section (WPA), one governing line (crease), visible structure counts (coptic), a baseline unit and no boxes (cracktro), snapping states (cathode). Code-led. Sketch: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned2.html`.
